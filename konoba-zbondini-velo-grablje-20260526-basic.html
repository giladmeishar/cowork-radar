A family konoba in the lavender village

Three families, a thousand lavender rows, one stone konoba lit by a fire most nights.

The kitchen, that morning

  • Peka of the day — lamb or octopus under the iron bell (book a day ahead)
  • Gregada — Dalmatian fishermen's stew, potato, olive oil, white wine
  • Antipasti of the island — pršut, paški sir, marinated olives
  • Plavac mali — local red wine, paired by the family
  • Lavender honey & ice cream — from the plateau below

How to find us

From Hvar town head north past Brusje. Velo Grablje is a 15-minute drive — pass the lavender distillery first. The konoba is the stone house with the open door.
