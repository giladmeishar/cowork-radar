The village

Three families, a thousand lavender rows.

Velo Grablje sits on a karst plateau between Hvar town and Stari Grad — once a lavender capital, today a near-empty hamlet of stone houses, terraced groves, and one konoba lit by a fire most nights.

The signature

The peka.

Lamb, octopus, or veal slow-cooked for hours under an iron bell heaped with embers — the dish you book a day ahead, the reason most people come up the hill.

How to find us

Up the switchbacks behind Brusje.

From Hvar town head north on the old road past Brusje. Velo Grablje is a 15-minute drive — you'll pass the lavender distillery first. The konoba is the stone house with the open door and the smell of a fire.

Reservation by phone or email. The peka needs notice.
