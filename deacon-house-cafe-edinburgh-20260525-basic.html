The close where Brodie lived both his lives

This is the close where William Brodie, hanged in 1788, kept a wax impression of every key in Edinburgh. Robert Louis Stevenson used him as the seed for Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde.

Many a citizen was proud of his close acquaintance with Deacon Brodie.— Robert Louis Stevenson
