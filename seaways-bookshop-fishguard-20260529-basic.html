— The shop

A blue door on West Street.

Seaways has been a working bookshop in Fishguard for over four decades — the pale-blue terrace house, the bunting outside in summer, the wooden shelves crammed floor to ceiling with art, fiction, children's books and second-hand finds.

No big chains. No algorithm. Just the slow joy of running a finger along spines, and a chair by the window if you fancy reading a chapter before deciding.

"A small shop run by people who actually read what they sell."
