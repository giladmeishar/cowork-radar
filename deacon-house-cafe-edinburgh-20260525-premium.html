The night side · 1788

Brodie was hanged on the gallows he designed himself.

This is the close where Deacon William Brodie lived: respected city councilor and master cabinet-maker by day, head of a burglary gang by night. He kept a wax impression of every key he could touch.

"He was hanged on a gallows of his own improvement — on the very 1st of October, in the year 1788."

Robert Louis Stevenson grew up around the corner and used Brodie as the seed for The Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde. The cafe sits in the very stone close, served by the same narrow staircases.
